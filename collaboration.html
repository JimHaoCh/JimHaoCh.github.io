03 / INTEGRATION & TESTING

串接晶片與操作系統Connect the chip to the experiment.

幫浦、閥件與電控整合

依流量、壓力、通道數及操作時序，規劃幫浦、閥件、管路接頭與控制介面，支援液體輸送、流路切換與自動化操作。

Pumps, valves, tubing and control interfaces are scoped around flow, pressure, channel count and timing for liquid delivery, switching and automated operation.

生物 wet bench 與初步測試

具生物實驗操作空間，可依合作範圍協助流動、混合、漏液及初步生物實驗測試。測試樣本、方法與交付結果於立案前討論確認。

Wet-bench facilities support preliminary flow, mixing, leakage and biological tests within the agreed scope. Samples, methods and deliverables are defined before the project starts.
