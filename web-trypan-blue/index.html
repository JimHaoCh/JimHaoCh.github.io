細胞存活率 Viability—

Cell viability (%) = Live ÷ (Live + Dead) × 100

待確認細胞不納入存活率分母；若存在待確認標記，數值為暫定結果。Unreviewed markers are excluded from the denominator. Results remain provisional until all markers are reviewed.

選用：計數盤濃度換算 Optional: Hemocytometer concentration

請先框選實際已知計數面積；完整 Neubauer 1 × 1 mm 大格，面積 1 mm²，標準深度 0.1 mm。若照片未校正計數區，請勿將結果作為細胞濃度。Select a physically known count area. A full Neubauer large square is 1 mm² at 0.1 mm depth. Uncalibrated fields cannot provide defensible cell concentrations.

染色原理 Principle

完整細胞膜可排除 Trypan Blue；膜完整性受損的細胞呈藍色。此方法量測的是染劑排除能力，結果受染色時間、細胞種類及顯微影像品質影響。Trypan Blue exclusion reflects membrane integrity. Dye uptake, cell type, staining time and imaging conditions affect classification.

方法與限制 Method & Limitations

使用局部亮度閾值、形態學與連通區篩選尋找候選細胞，再以中心區域 B−R 色差推估藍色染色。相鄰細胞、離焦、染色沉澱及格線可能造成漏計或誤計；人工標記會保留在匯出檔。Local contrast, morphology and connected components detect candidates. Central blue chroma supports provisional classification. Overlaps, defocus, debris and grid lines remain sources of error.
