MASTER'S TRAINING ROADMAP

碩士班培訓與畢業路徑 Master’s Training & Graduation Roadmap

以臨床體外診斷（IVD）為主軸，從研究問題、晶片原型到整合與驗證，逐步完成碩士研究。From diagnostic needs to device prototypes, system integration, and research validation.

◈

微型化 Microfluidics & Miniaturization

微流體、晶片製程、樣本處理與定點檢測Chip fabrication, sample handling, and point-of-care testing

◎

多重偵測 Multiplex Diagnostics

細菌、癌症生物標記、核酸、蛋白質與小分子Bacteria, biomarkers, nucleic acids, proteins, and small molecules

▧

整合 AI AI-assisted Diagnostics

影像分析、ImageJ、電腦視覺與適用題目的 AI 模型Image analysis, computer vision, and project-appropriate AI models

  1. 碩一上 Year 1 · Fall

    目標建立 Define the research goal

    臨床需求、文獻閱讀、研究題目與基本實驗能力Clinical needs, literature, project selection, and foundational skills

  2. 碩一下 Year 1 · Spring

    手段方法 Develop methods

    微流體或感測器原型、檢測方法與初步性能評估Prototype development, assay design, and initial performance testing

  3. 暑假至碩二上 Summer – Year 2 · Fall

    整合與驗證 Integrate & validate

    參數最佳化、裝置整合、重複性與實際情境測試Optimization, device integration, repeatability, and application-oriented evaluation

  4. 碩二下 Year 2 · Spring

    完成研究 Complete the research

    資料分析、碩士論文、研究成果發表與後續發展Data analysis, thesis, research dissemination, and next steps

實際進度依題目、實驗結果及系所畢業規定調整。涉及人體檢體或臨床合作時，須依需求取得 IRB 等必要核准；期刊投稿與臨床驗證依研究成果安排。Milestones depend on the project, results, and degree requirements. Human-subject research requires relevant approvals when applicable; publications and clinical validation depend on project scope.
