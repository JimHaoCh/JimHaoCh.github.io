MASTER'S TRAINING ROADMAP · IVD DEVELOPMENT

碩士班培訓與畢業路徑Master’s Training & Graduation Roadmap

以臨床體外診斷（IVD）設備為研究主軸，從選擇檢測目標、建立微流體與生物感測方法，到裝置整合、性能評估及研究成果撰寫，規劃兩年研究路徑。Our IVD-oriented research pathway spans diagnostic target selection, microfluidics and biosensing methods, prototype integration, performance evaluation, and scientific writing.

◈

微型化Miniaturization

將樣本處理與分析流程整合進微流體或微感測器；學習通道設計、晶片材料選擇、CNC／3D 列印、微奈米製程及流體控制。Miniaturize sample handling and detection through microfluidic design, material selection, fabrication, and flow control.

◎

多重偵測Multiplex Diagnostics

依臨床需求確立微生物、癌症標記、目標基因、蛋白質或小分子等分析物；探索陣列式、平行通道及數位化檢測與訊號讀出。Choose clinically relevant microbial or cancer biomarkers, genes, proteins, or small molecules, and explore arrayed, parallel, or digital assays.

▧

整合 AIAI-assisted Diagnostics

建立影像判讀、資料標註與數據分析能力，循序學習 ImageJ、Python 及視需要採用 CNN、ResNet 或 YOLO；練習使用合適的開源專案與資料集。Develop image interpretation and analysis skills with ImageJ, Python, and, where appropriate, CNN/ResNet/YOLO and open-source datasets.

  1. 碩一上Year 1 · Fall

    目標建立Define the Goal

    閱讀臨床 IVD 與相關技術文獻，選定微流體／微感測器應用情境、目標分析物及預期性能指標。建立基本操作、研究紀錄及實驗設計能力。Review IVD literature; define the clinical use case, analyte, performance targets, and core experimental skills.

  2. 碩一下Year 1 · Spring

    手段方法與原型Methods & Prototyping

    開發陣列式或數位化微流體與感測器原型，進行生物辨識、訊號讀出及測試流程建立。依研究需要整合光學／電學量測、影像演算法或 DNA 電路放大等技術。Build microfluidic/biosensor prototypes; establish recognition and readout workflows, optionally using optical/electrical sensing, imaging, or DNA circuit amplification.

  3. 暑假至碩二上Summer – Year 2 · Fall

    整合、優化與驗證Integration & Validation

    整合晶片、流體控制、感測及影像分析；調整關鍵參數，檢驗靈敏度、特異性、檢測極限、重複性與裝置穩定性。完成預定功能與性能指標，整理研討會資料。Integrate subsystems, optimize parameters, and evaluate sensitivity, specificity, detection limits, repeatability, stability, and predefined performance targets.

  4. 碩二下Year 2 · Spring

    研究完成與臨床轉譯Research Completion & Translation

    完成確認性實驗、統計分析及碩士論文。依研究成果安排學術論文投稿、臨床合作單位測試或進一步確效；涉及人體研究或可識別檢體時，依法規及合作機構要求辦理 IRB 等審查。Complete confirmatory experiments, analysis, and the thesis. Manuscripts or clinical collaborations depend on results; human-subject work follows applicable ethics approvals.

研究進度的兩項檢核Two Research Milestones

01 · 參數最佳化建立合適的對照組、測量條件及重複性驗證，據以調整晶片與分析方法。Optimize operating parameters using controls, defined conditions, and repeatability testing.

02 · 裝置達成目標依事先訂定的規格，確認功能及性能指標達到研究預期，並記錄尚待改善的限制。Verify the device against predefined functional and analytical requirements, and document limitations.

以上為預期培訓進度，依個別研究題目、實驗結果、設備條件與系所畢業規範調整。正式臨床確效、IRB、期刊接受與專利成果均依研究需要及相關審核程序辦理。This is a planning roadmap, not a guaranteed graduation schedule. Formal clinical validation, IRB approval, publications, and IP follow the project's needs and applicable processes.
