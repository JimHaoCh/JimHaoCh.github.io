加入實驗室與碩士訓練Joining the Lab & Master’s Training

沒有工程背景或程式能力，可以加入嗎？Can I join without an engineering or programming background?

歡迎醫技、生科、生醫及相關領域學生洽詢。醫技背景能幫助理解檢體、檢驗流程、品質控制與臨床需求；晶片設計、基礎製程、影像分析和程式工具可透過研究訓練循序學習。加入前建議具備基本實驗紀錄習慣、文獻閱讀意願，以及願意嘗試跨領域問題的態度。

Applicants from medical laboratory science, biotechnology, biomedical science, and related backgrounds are welcome. Clinical laboratory knowledge supports sample handling, quality control, and use-case definition. Engineering and coding skills can be developed during training.

加入後如何開始研究？能在兩年內完成嗎？How does training begin, and can the program be completed in two years?

預計利用暑假進行約 2–3 週的基礎設備與實驗流程訓練，包括實驗安全、紀錄方式、晶片或影像工具入門。碩一上確認研究問題及檢測目標，碩一下建立原型和測試方法，暑假至碩二上進行參數最佳化及整合驗證，碩二下完成論文與口試準備。兩年為研究規劃目標，實際畢業須符合校系修課與審查規定，並依個人研究進度調整。

The plan begins with approximately 2–3 weeks of summer orientation in safety, documentation, equipment, and introductory chip/imaging tools. Students then progress from target definition to prototypes, validation, and thesis preparation. Graduation timing follows research progress and degree regulations.

會學到哪些技術？每個人都要做 AI 嗎？Which technical skills are taught? Is AI required for every project?

可能接觸微流體設計、簡易晶片製程（如 CNC、3D 列印與模具製作）、生物感測與檢驗試劑開發、訊號量測、影像處理、資料分析，以及 Python 或 AI 輔助程式開發（Vibe Coding）。若研究包含影像辨識，可進一步評估 ImageJ、CNN、ResNet、YOLO 或公開資料集；是否使用深度學習取決於研究問題、資料量與所需的驗證方式。

Depending on the project, training may include microfluidics, CNC/3D-printed prototypes, biosensing, reagents, signal acquisition, imaging, and Python/AI-assisted coding. Deep learning tools such as CNN, ResNet, or YOLO are selected when appropriate to the question and available data.

除了實驗技術，還會培養哪些軟實力？Which transferable skills will I develop?

訓練包含文獻搜尋與判讀、研究假說與實驗設計、對照組與資料品質管理、專案進度追蹤、團隊協作、問題排查，以及中英文簡報、圖表製作和論文撰寫。學生需要能解釋實驗結果及其限制，並將下一步工作轉化為可執行的計畫。

Students practice literature review, hypothesis-driven experimental design, controls, data quality, project tracking, troubleshooting, teamwork, scientific presentations, figures, and manuscript writing, including clear interpretation of study limitations.

畢業後可以從事哪些工作或繼續升學？What careers or further study paths are available?

研究訓練可銜接體外診斷（IVD）與臨床檢驗設備公司、醫療器材研發、生物感測與微流體相關企業、半導體和生醫交叉領域的研發職務、研究機構，以及國內外博士班。實際職缺仍取決於個人專題、技能組合、求職條件與產業需求。具轉譯潛力的成果可另評估產學合作、專利或技術移轉。

Potential paths include IVD and laboratory equipment companies, medical-device R&D, biosensing/microfluidics, biomedical–semiconductor engineering, research institutes, and doctoral study. Opportunities depend on each graduate’s skills and the job market; suitable projects may also lead to industry collaboration or IP evaluation.
