加入實驗室與碩士訓練 Joining the Lab & Master’s Training

沒有工程背景可以加入嗎？Can I join without an engineering background?

歡迎醫學檢驗、生物科技、生命科學及相關領域學生洽詢。研究訓練會依個人背景與題目，逐步涵蓋微流體、感測器、晶片製程與影像分析。

Students from medical laboratory science, biotechnology, life sciences, and related fields are welcome to inquire. Training in microfluidics, biosensors, fabrication, and image analysis is tailored to each project.

加入後如何開始研究？何時能畢業？How does training begin, and what is the graduation timeline?

預計於暑假安排約 2–3 週的基礎訓練與設備操作，逐步確立研究題目。以兩年完成碩士研究為規劃目標，實際畢業時間依研究進度與校系規定。

An introductory summer training period of approximately 2–3 weeks is planned for foundational techniques and equipment. The roadmap targets a two-year master’s program; actual graduation follows research progress and university requirements.

碩士期間會學到哪些技術？What technical skills will I learn?

依題目接觸簡易晶片製程、微流體設計、生物檢測、檢驗試劑與醫材開發、影像分析、資料處理及 AI 輔助程式開發（Vibe Coding）。各項技術依研究需求安排。

Depending on the project, students may learn chip fabrication, microfluidic design, biological assays, diagnostic reagent and device development, image analysis, data processing, and AI-assisted coding.

除了實驗操作，會培養哪些能力？What skills beyond laboratory techniques are developed?

包含研究設計、問題拆解、專案管理、文獻閱讀、成果報告、口語與書面表達，以及有效率的實驗規劃。

Training emphasizes experimental design, problem solving, project management, literature reading, scientific communication, and efficient experiment planning.

畢業後有哪些發展方向？What career paths can graduates pursue?

可探索 IVD 與檢驗設備商、醫材與醫療科技產業、生醫與半導體跨域研發、研究機構及國內外博士班。實際職涯取決於個人能力、興趣與職缺條件。

Possible paths include IVD and laboratory equipment companies, medical-device and health-technology industries, interdisciplinary biomedical/semiconductor R&D, research institutes, and doctoral studies.
