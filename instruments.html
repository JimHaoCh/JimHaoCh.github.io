實驗儀器Instruments

實驗室設備涵蓋微流體元件製作、精密流體控制、顯微影像、生物材料列印、表面處理及一般生物實驗。各設備依研究目的與操作規範安排使用。

Our facilities support microfluidic fabrication, precision flow control, microscopy, biomaterial printing, surface treatment and routine biological experiments. Equipment is used according to the research purpose and laboratory procedures.
