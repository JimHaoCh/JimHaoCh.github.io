FOCUS 01 · CYTOTECHNOLOGY

細胞醫檢師Cytotechnologist

從細胞形態判讀與篩檢著手，再查詢台灣病理學會當年度甄訓公告。國際 IAC 認證另有工作年資與考試規定。

Begin with cytomorphology and screening, then review current Taiwan training announcements. IAC certification has separate eligibility requirements.

FOCUS 02 · CLINICAL EMBRYOLOGY

胚胎師Clinical Embryologist

從生殖細胞、胚胎培養及精液檢驗認識人工生殖實驗室，再查詢國健署認定的技術員訓練機構及現行法規。

Study gametes and embryo culture, then review HPA-recognized training centres and Taiwanese ART laboratory rules.
