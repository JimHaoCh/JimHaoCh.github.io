判讀原理 Method

本版根據 RGB 色差尋找紫色及粉紅色色塊，再用連通區面積及二階矩（major/minor axis ratio）粗略區分球形與桿形。形狀臨界區與過大候選會標示待確認；結果隨門檻、背景及染色而變動。RGB chromatic contrast detects purple/pink pixels. Connected components and second-moment axis ratios offer provisional cocci/rod suggestions. Ambiguous shapes are flagged for review.

臨床限制 Scope & Limitations

這個簡化模型對真實抹片的染色沉澱、厚薄差、背景細胞與密集群聚缺乏驗證。學生仍需辨識菌體大小、排列、染色品質及檢體背景。未包含病原菌種、Gram-variable、真菌、混合菌群的可靠推論，亦未提供染色品質分數。The detector lacks validation on clinical smears. Precipitates, dense growth, cell debris, and variations in stain quality can confound predictions. Manual microscope review remains essential.
